# Privacy notice skeleton — SplashTrack

**What this is.** A skeleton for the privacy notice your organisation publishes
(Art. 13/14 GDPR). The bracketed parts are yours. The unbracketed parts describe
what the software actually does and are safe to keep as written.

**What this is not.** Legal advice, and not a notice you can publish unchanged.
The project is not your processor and cannot draft your notice (F-27, F-126).

The factual source for every list below is `docs/design/01-domain-model.md` §5
and `docs/privacy/dpia-template.md` §1. Where the two disagree, the domain model
wins — it is what the software enforces.

## Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of everything we hold about you or your child, ask us
to correct it, ask us to delete it, object to some uses, and withdraw any
consent you gave. Withdrawing consent does not undo what we did before you
withdrew it.

When we give you a copy, it includes everything we hold, who has received it,
how long we keep each part, and where it came from. If any part cannot be
included, we tell you rather than quietly leaving it out.

Some things we must keep even if you ask us to delete them — for example
`[financial records for the tax authority]` and our security log, which records
who changed what. When that happens we tell you what we kept and why.

If your child is `[16]` or older, these rights are theirs rather than yours, and
we will ask them to confirm consents that were previously given on their behalf.
